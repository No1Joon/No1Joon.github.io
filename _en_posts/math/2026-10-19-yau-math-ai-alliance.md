A math AI research alliance led by Fields Medalist **Shing-Tung Yau** (丘成桐) launched at Tsinghua University in Beijing on October 11
Eight Chinese mathematics research institutions are taking part, and it said it expects about <mark>1,000</mark> researchers to participate

In June of the same year, mathematicians released the **Leiden Declaration**, calling for verification of AI proofs and disclosure of tools
The two moves differ in how they approach AI math research

## Makeup of the alliance

Its official name is the Yau Laboratory Alliance for Mathematics and AI
The participating institutions reported by Science and Technology Daily (科技日报) on October 13 are the eight below

- Qiuzhen College (求真书院), Tsinghua University
- Yau Mathematical Sciences Center, Tsinghua University
- Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA)
- Morningside Center of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences
- Shanghai Institute for Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences
- Yau Center, Southeast University (Nanjing)
- Hetao Institute of Mathematics and Interdisciplinary Sciences (Shenzhen)
- Chih-Chen College, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Yau heads the alliance, and academic leads nominated by each institution set research directions and evaluation criteria in a committee
Participating institutions share large-scale model training resources, **formalized mathematics libraries**, and computing platforms

A plan to set up math AI institutes in five places, Beijing, Shanghai, Nanjing, Shenzhen, and Hong Kong, was announced as well

## Goals and current stage

The goals are math-specialized AI models, AI research infrastructure, interdisciplinary research, and talent development
VnExpress and others also reported proposing new mathematical ideas and generating proofs researchers can verify as goals

No models or results have been released yet
The 1,000 figure was also announced not as current headcount but as <mark>the number expected to participate in the future</mark>

### 🗣️ Yau's diagnosis

Yau said Chinese AI models do everyday tasks well
But he said they lag far behind leading US systems in advanced mathematics and physics

His explanation is that US companies mobilize thousands of researchers while China's research workforce is scattered across many organizations

According to SCMP, he said that a year ago he didn't know AI would be this powerful
He compared AI's progress in mathematics to a tsunami

## The Leiden Declaration's principles for AI verification

![Leiden Declaration homepage with the International Mathematical Union (IMU) endorsement](/assets/images/math/yau-math-ai-alliance/02-photo.webp)
*Leiden Declaration homepage with the International Mathematical Union (IMU) endorsement — Source: Leiden Declaration*

The Leiden Declaration was released on June 2, 2026, and endorsed by the **International Mathematical Union (IMU)**
Sixteen researchers who gathered at a workshop at the Lorentz Center in Leiden, the Netherlands, in September 2025 wrote the draft

According to the declaration's site, there are <mark>4,357</mark> signatories as of October 19
It doesn't call for banning AI use, but asks for the following

| Principle | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| Verification | Automatically generated proofs are subject to **independent verification** |
| Disclosure | State the AI tools used in research |
| Attribution | If attribution isn't possible, say so explicitly |

IMU Vice President Ulrike Tillmann said mathematics should remain a deeply human activity
