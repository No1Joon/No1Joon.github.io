# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This site (cailmeal.com) has no sign-up, comments, or contact forms, and does not directly collect personal information such as names or email addresses.

## Visitor statistics

This site uses **Google Analytics 4** for visitor statistics. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect the following.

- Pages visited, time of visit, referral source
- Browser and device type, approximate location (country or city level)

This information is used only as statistics that do not identify individuals, and it is processed under Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Advertising

This site may show **Google AdSense** ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

- You can turn off personalized ads in [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- How Google uses data for ads is described in [Google's advertising policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Refusing cookies

You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings. Refusing cookies does not affect reading the posts.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please reach out through my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/No1Joon).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
